# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- testing Liaison against a non-consenting person, phone number, or account;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and whenever an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers using private contact information on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not put sensitive reports or personal data in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of the reporter as far as reasonably possible. Enforcement may include a correction, warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban, depending on impact and recurrence.
